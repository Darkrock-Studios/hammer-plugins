Name lists

Each list has a name and how many people have it on each line, most common first.
hammer-plugins' real-names/tools/ builds them, a script for each culture.

american/

feminine.txt, masculine.txt, and the lists by decade of birth hold the 1,000 most common given names
of each sex among Social Security card applications for births in the United States, over 1880 to
the latest year published and within each decade. From the Social Security Administration's national
baby names data, https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/limits.html. It gives names without spaces,
hyphens, or capitals inside them. A name over 20 times as common for the other sex is left out.

surnames.txt holds the 5,000 most common surnames of the 2010 census. From the Census Bureau's
Frequently Occurring Surnames from the 2010 Census,
https://www.census.gov/topics/population/genealogy/data/2010_surnames.html. It gives surnames in
capitals without spaces or punctuation; the script restores those of the common ones.

Both are works of the United States government, in the public domain.
