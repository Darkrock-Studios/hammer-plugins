Name lists

Each list has a name and how many people have it on each line, most common first.
hammer-plugins' real-names/tools/ builds them, a script for each culture.

american/

feminine.txt, masculine.txt, and the lists by decade of birth hold the 1,000 most common given names
of each sex among Social Security card applications for births in the United States, over 1880 to
the latest year published and within each decade. From the Social Security Administration's national
baby names data, https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/limits.html. It gives names without spaces,
hyphens, or capitals inside them. A name over 20 times as common for the other sex is left out.

surnames.txt holds the 5,000 most common surnames of the 2010 census. From the Census Bureau's
Frequently Occurring Surnames from the 2010 Census,
https://www.census.gov/topics/population/genealogy/data/2010_surnames.html. It gives surnames in
capitals without spaces or punctuation; the script restores those of the common ones.

Both are works of the United States government, in the public domain.

french/

feminine.txt, masculine.txt, and the lists by decade of birth hold the 1,000 most common given names
of each sex among births in France, over 1900 to 2022 and within each decade. From INSEE's Fichier des
prénoms, 2022 edition, https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/7633685. A name over 20 times as common
for the other sex is left out.

surnames.txt holds the 5,000 most common surnames among births in France from 1891 to 2000. From
INSEE's Fichier des noms de famille, 2008 edition, https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/3536630. It
gives surnames in capitals without accents; the script restores those of the common ones.

Both are shared by INSEE under the Licence Ouverte 2.0, https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/.

english/

The lists by decade of birth since the 1990s hold the 1,000 most common given names of each sex among
births in England and Wales from 1996 to 2025. From the Office for National Statistics' Baby names in
England and Wales: from 1996, shared under the Open Government Licence v3.0,
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/. A name over 20 times as
common for the other sex is left out. The other lists are from Wikidata, below.

irish/

feminine.txt, masculine.txt, and the lists by decade of birth since the 1960s hold the 1,000 most
common given names of each sex among births registered in Ireland from 1964 to 2024, and surnames.txt
the surnames of babies of 2023 and 2024. From the Central Statistics Office's tables VSA50, VSA60, and
VSA110, https://data.cso.ie, shared under CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. A
name over 20 times as common for the other sex is left out. Lists for earlier decades are from
Wikidata, below.

norwegian/

feminine.txt, masculine.txt, and the lists by decade of birth hold the most common given names of each
sex among those born in Norway from 1880 to 2025, each with how many of 100,000 born it was given to,
a year at a time, and surnames.txt the surnames 200 people or more have. From Statistics Norway's
StatBank tables 10467 and 12891, https://www.ssb.no/en/statbank, shared under CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. A name over 20 times as common for the other sex is left
out.

polish/

feminine.txt and masculine.txt hold the 1,000 most common first given names of each sex in Poland's
PESEL register, and surnames-feminine.txt and surnames-masculine.txt the 5,000 most common surnames of
each, the dead counted too. From the lists the Ministry of Digital Affairs shares at
https://dane.gov.pl, datasets 1501 and 568, in the public domain (CC0). The lists by decade of birth
are from Wikidata, below.

scottish/

feminine.txt, masculine.txt, and the lists by decade of birth since the 1970s hold the 1,000 most
common given names of each sex among births registered in Scotland from 1974 to 2025, and surnames.txt
the surnames in its registers of births, marriages, and deaths in every fifth year from 1975 to 2010
and each year since 2013. From National Records of Scotland's Babies' First Names and Most Common
Surnames, https://www.nrscotland.gov.uk, shared under the Open Government Licence v3.0,
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/. A name over 20 times as
common for the other sex is left out. Lists for earlier decades are from Wikidata, below.

The lists hold the first given names and the family names of each country's people born since 1880
that Wikidata, https://www.wikidata.org, has, by their English labels, and how many of those people
have each: names at least 3 have, and a decade's list where it has at least 500 people and 50 names. A
country's people are its citizens, and those of the states before it born where it is now; England's
are the United Kingdom's. Japanese and Chinese names are without the marks on their letters. Greek,
Russian, and Ukrainian surnames are counted for both sexes together, and a woman's formed from a man's
by its ending. They are the names of people of note, so follow the population's only roughly.
Wikidata's data is in the public domain (CC0).
