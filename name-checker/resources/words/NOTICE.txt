Word lists other than English

fr.txt, de.txt, es.txt, it.txt, and uk.txt hold the words among the 200,000 most frequent of a
subtitle frequency list that a spell-check dictionary accepts in lower case, sorted by byte. German
also keeps the words its dictionary has capitalized as nouns: those whose entries have a noun's
flags, or that no other language's dictionary knows only capitalized, as a name. Ukrainian keeps
only words in Cyrillic. hammer-plugins' name-checker/tools/word-lists.py builds them. Each list
is shared under the terms of both its sources.

Frequency lists: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, from OpenSubtitles 2018,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).

  https://grammalecte.net/. Licensed MPL 2.0 (licenses/MPL-2.0.txt).
- de: igerman98 with the frami additions, by Björn Jacke and Franz Michael Baumann,
  https://www.j3e.de/ispell/igerman98/. Licensed GPL version 2 or 3 (licenses/GPL-2.0.txt,
  licenses/GPL-3.0.txt).
- es: the RLA-ES Spanish dictionary (es_ES), https://github.com/sbosio/rla-es. Licensed, at your
  choice, GPL 3 or later, LGPL 3 or later, or MPL 1.1 or later (licenses/GPL-3.0.txt,
  licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt, licenses/MPL-1.1.txt).
- it: the Italian Writing Aids extension 5.1.1, by LibreItalia, Marina Latini, and others,

- uk: from dict_uk, by Andriy Rysin and others, https://github.com/brown-uk/dict_uk. Licensed MPL 1.1
