## Character
Questions from the confession album Marcel Proust filled in around 1890, put to your character.

- What is the main trait of your character?
- What quality do you most want in a man?
- What quality do you most want in a woman?
- What do you value most in your friends?
- What is your chief fault?
- What is your favourite occupation?
- What is your dream of happiness?

## Hopes and losses
- What would be your greatest misfortune?
- What would you like to be?
- In what country would you like to live?
- What gift of nature would you most like to have?
- How would you like to die?
- What is your present state of mind?
- For what faults do you have the most tolerance?

## Tastes
- What is your favourite colour?
- What is your favourite flower?
- What is your favourite bird?
- Who are your favourite writers?
- Who are your favourite poets?
- Who are your favourite composers?
- Who are your favourite painters?

## Heroes
- Who are your heroes in fiction?
- Who are your heroines in fiction?
- Who are your heroes in real life?
- Who are your heroines in history?
- What are your favourite names?
- Which figures in history do you despise most?

## Judgements
- What do you hate above all else?
- What military deed do you most admire?
- What reform do you most admire?
- What is your motto?
