## The basics
Picture them sitting across from you. Answer as they would, or in your own words about them.

- What's your full name, and what do your friends call you?
- How old are you, and how old do you feel?
- Where do you live, and who with?
- What do you do for work, and do you like it?

## Appearance
- What do people notice first about you?
- How do you dress, and why?
- How do you carry yourself when you walk into a room?
- What do you like least about how you look?

## Background
- Where did you grow up, and what was home like?
- Who raised you, and what did they teach you without meaning to?
- What's your fondest memory?
- What's the memory you'd rather forget?

## Personality
- What makes you laugh?
- What makes you lose your temper?
- What's a habit you can't break?
- What do you do when no one is watching?

## Wants and needs
- What do you want more than anything?
- What do you need, even if you don't know it yet?
- What stands in your way?
- What happens if you never get it?

## Fears and wounds
- What are you most afraid of?
- What happened to you that still hurts?
- What lie do you believe about yourself or the world?
- What's your greatest regret?
- What secret are you keeping?

## Relationships
- Who do you trust most, and why?
- Who do you love, and do they know?
- Who is your rival, or your enemy?
- Who would you drop everything to help?

## Voice and change
- How do you talk? Any words or phrases you use too often?
- What would you never say out loud?
- What's your motto, spoken or not?
- Who are you at the start of this story, and who might you be at the end?
