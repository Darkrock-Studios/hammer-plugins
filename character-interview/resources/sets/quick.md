## Who they are
Picture them sitting across from you. Answer as they would, or in your own words about them.

- What do people call you, and what do you wish they called you?
- What do you do with your days?
- How would a stranger describe you after five minutes?

## Wants and fears
- What do you want more than anything?
- What would you never do, even to get it?
- What are you most afraid of?

## Wounds and secrets
- What happened to you that still hurts?
- What do you believe about yourself because of it?
- What are you hiding, and from whom?

## Voice
- How do you talk when you're angry? When you're lying?
- What do you say too often?
- What's your motto, spoken or not?
