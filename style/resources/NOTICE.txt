Word lists from other sources

fr/not-adverbs.txt, es/not-adverbs.txt, and it/not-adverbs.txt hold the words among the 100,000 most
frequent of a subtitle frequency list that end in the language's adverb suffix but that its spell-check
dictionary does not take for adverbs. hammer-plugins' style/tools/not-adverbs.py builds them. Each list
is shared under the terms of both its sources. The other lists here are hammer-plugins' own.

Frequency lists: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, from OpenSubtitles 2018,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).

Dictionaries, from https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries:
- fr: Dictionnaires orthographiques français 7.7, by Olivier R. and contributors,
  https://grammalecte.net/. Licensed MPL 2.0 (licenses/MPL-2.0.txt).
- es: the RLA-ES Spanish dictionary (es_ES), https://github.com/sbosio/rla-es. Licensed, at your
  choice, GPL 3 or later, LGPL 3 or later, or MPL 1.1 or later (licenses/GPL-3.0.txt,
  licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt, licenses/MPL-1.1.txt).
- it: the Italian Writing Aids extension 5.1.1, by LibreItalia, Marina Latini, and others,
  https://libreitalia.org. Licensed GPL 3 (licenses/GPL-3.0.txt).
