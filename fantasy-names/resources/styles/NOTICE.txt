Name lists

Each style's lists hold real names, a name on each line, for the names the plugin makes up to sound
like. hammer-plugins' fantasy-names/tools/build.py builds them. A list leaves out names of more than
one word, and names among today's American ones.

elvish/ holds Welsh given names; high-elvish/ Finnish given names, and Finnish surnames as bynames,
without those that look borrowed; dwarvish/ Old Norse given names, and Icelandic ones made of Norse
parts, written as English writes them, Haraldr as Harald; and old-kingdom/ Old English given names,
with th and ae for the letters English has lost.

The given names are from the given-name categories of Wiktionary, https://en.wiktionary.org, by its
contributors, shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 (licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt), as these lists are. The Welsh and
Finnish lists add the names Wikidata, https://www.wikidata.org, has of each language, and the bynames
are Wikidata's; its data is in the public domain (CC0).

everyday.txt holds the 1,000 most common given names of each sex among Social Security card
applications for births in the United States since 1880, in lower case, from the Social Security
Administration's national baby names data, https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/limits.html, a work of
the United States government in the public domain.
