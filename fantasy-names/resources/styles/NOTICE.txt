Name lists

Each style's lists hold real names, a name on each line, for the names the plugin makes up to sound
like. hammer-plugins' fantasy-names/tools/build.py builds them. A list leaves out names of more than
one word, and names among today's American ones.

elvish/ holds Welsh given names; high-elvish/ Finnish given names, and Finnish surnames as bynames,
without those that look borrowed; dwarvish/ Old Norse given names, and Icelandic ones made of Norse
parts, written as English writes them, Haraldr as Harald; old-kingdom/ Old English given names, with
th and ae for the letters English has lost; imperial/ the family names of Rome and its first names,
a woman's being a family's with her ending, and its cognomina as bynames; highland/ Old, Middle, and
modern Irish and Scottish Gaelic given names, without the marks on their letters; and forest/ Czech,
Slovak, Serbo-Croatian, Polish, and Slovene given names made of Slavic parts, without those marks.

Those names are from the categories of Wiktionary, https://en.wiktionary.org, by its contributors,
shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 (licenses/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt), as these lists are. The Welsh, Finnish, and
Irish lists add the given names Wikidata, https://www.wikidata.org, has of each language, and the
Finnish bynames are its family names.

mythic/ holds the names of those in Greek myth, and of the cities of ancient Greece as places, as
Wikidata has them. Its data is in the public domain (CC0).

orcish/ is the plugin's own, its names written for it.

everyday.txt holds the 1,000 most common given names of each sex among Social Security card
applications for births in the United States since 1880, in lower case, from the Social Security
Administration's national baby names data, https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/limits.html, a work of
the United States government in the public domain.
